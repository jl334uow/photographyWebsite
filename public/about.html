JUSTIN LU PHOTOGRAPHY

Light, movement, and moments in between

I’m a Sydney-based photographer focused on travel, landscape, and street photography—capturing moments as they naturally unfold.

I first picked up a camera in high school, driven by a simple goal: to take better photos and preserve moments I could look back on. That mindset still shapes how I shoot today. My work is less about staging and more about observing—finding composition, light, and emotion in real environments.

My photography is heavily influenced by movement and exploration. As a long-distance runner, I spend a lot of time outdoors, often discovering new locations and perspectives along the way. That sense of motion carries into my work—whether it’s a quiet street scene, a changing skyline, or a landscape at a specific moment in time.

Alongside photography, I come from a background in software and data engineering. That technical perspective influences how I approach my work—through attention to detail, structure, and continuous improvement—while still leaving room for creativity.

Previously, I’ve also worked in event photography, capturing candid moments in fast-paced environments such as birthday celebrations.

This site is a collection of moments I’ve captured across different places and times.

I’m currently open to photography work and collaborations. Prints will also be available soon.
